Frequently asked questions

Is Convene free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and registering for events is free.

What happens when an event is full?

Registration closes automatically once capacity is reached, so every registered attendee is guaranteed a seat.

Can I cancel a registration?

Yes. Open My Registrations and click Cancel. Your seat is released for someone else.

How does check-in work?

Event staff open the check-in list for the event and mark you as arrived. The time of arrival is recorded.

Can other apps connect to Convene?

Yes. Convene has a REST API with token authentication for listing events and managing registrations.
